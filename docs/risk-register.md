# Risk register

Engine: B, repackaging. Scale: Likelihood (L) and Impact (I) are 1 (low) to 3 (high).
Score = L × I. Review at the end of every phase.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Score | Mitigation | Verify via | Phase |
|----|------|---|---|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| R1 | Popular apps (WhatsApp, Instagram, banking) don't work as clones: signature checks, Play Integrity, Google Sign-In/FCM bound to package + SHA-1. | 3 | 3 | 9 | Honest compatibility hints in the picker, bundled compatibility list from spike S3, explainer page, recommend the app's built-in multi-account feature. Never bypass. | S3 | 2, 3, 6 |
| R2 | Account ban for users of apps that treat re-signed clients as unofficial (WhatsApp, Meta). | 2 | 3 | 6 | Mandatory warning dialog before cloning those packages. Disclaimer. Not offered as "recommended". | UX review | 3, 6 |
| R3 | Binary patching bugs produce APKs that fail to install (`INSTALL_PARSE_FAILED_*`) or crash at start. | 2 | 3 | 6 | Host JVM tests: aapt2-built fixtures, round-trip parse/serialize equality, `apksigner verify`, `zipalign -c -P 16`. Errors mapped to clear Indonesian messages. Work directory always cleaned. | S1, S2 | 3 |
| R4 | Losing a clone's signing key (Kembar uninstalled / data cleared) means the clone can't be updated, and its data is lost when it has to be reinstalled. | 2 | 3 | 6 | Encrypted key backup with reminders. Key file in `noBackupFilesDir` (never in cloud backup). Clear explanation in onboarding and in Detail Clone. | Phase 5 tests | 3, 5 |
| R5 | Play Protect warns or blocks installs of repackaged well-known apps. | 2 | 2 | 4 | Explain in the install step. Never advise disabling Play Protect. Record behaviour in S3. | S3 | 3 |
| R6 | Insufficient storage (clone APK copies 100–300 MB each, ~2× during build). | 2 | 2 | 4 | Analyzer checks free space first. Streaming writes. Cleanup on every exit path. Storage shown per clone. | Unit + manual | 3 |
| R7 | Long clone jobs killed by OEM battery managers (Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo). | 2 | 2 | 4 | Foreground `dataSync` worker with an expedited request. Idempotent stages with clean restart. Help article on battery settings per OEM. | Manual on OEMs | 3 |
| R8 | Android 15+ limits `dataSync` foreground services (6 h per 24 h) or future releases tighten FGS types. | 1 | 2 | 2 | Jobs take minutes, not hours. Handle `onTimeout` by failing the job cleanly. Review behaviour changes each release. | CI emulator | 3, 7 |
| R9 | Exported component / intent spoofing (another app triggers clone creation, launches, or the install-result receiver). | 2 | 3 | 6 | Install-result receiver not exported, explicit `PendingIntent`s only, `FLAG_MUTABLE` only where the system must fill extras. No exported activities except the launcher and the pinned-shortcut trampoline. The trampoline only accepts clone IDs that exist in Room and enforces the app lock. | Phase 6 audit + tests | 3, 4, 6 |
| R10 | Malicious guest app: a cloned app is as trustworthy as the original, but now has a new signer. | 1 | 2 | 2 | Kembar only clones apps already installed by the user. Each clone has its own key, so clones can't share signature permissions with each other or with Kembar. | Design | 3 |
| R11 | Tampered clone: someone replaces an installed clone with a different APK under the same package. | 1 | 2 | 2 | Health check compares the installed signer SHA-256 with the stored `signer_cert_sha256` and marks a mismatch BROKEN. Android blocks such updates anyway unless the clone is uninstalled first. | Phase 5 | 5 |
| R12 | Backup leakage (key backup or settings export read by someone else). | 2 | 3 | 6 | AES-256-GCM, passphrase-derived key (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 310 000 iterations, random salt). `allowBackup=false` + `dataExtractionRules` exclude everything. No plaintext export path. | Phase 5 tests | 1, 5 |
| R13 | Users confuse clone and original. | 2 | 2 | 4 | System label "… · Kembar n" patched into the manifest. Badge on Kembar's list and pinned shortcuts. Icon badge pending S6. | S6 | 3, 4 |
| R14 | Legal / ToS: some apps forbid multiple accounts or modified clients. | 2 | 2 | 4 | Disclaimer must be accepted. Per-app warnings. Kembar never hides clone status. | Phase 1, 6 | 1, 6 |
| R15 | Pinned toolchain (AGP 8.5.2, compileSdk 34) ages: targetSdk 34 is below Play's current minimum and misses newer platform APIs. | 3 | 1 | 3 | Accepted for sideload distribution. Upgrade path documented in Phase 7 (AGP/compileSdk bump is mechanical). | Phase 7 | 7 |
| R16 | Per-clone network toggle via local VPN conflicts with the user's own VPN. | 2 | 1 | 2 | Gated by S7. Optional, clearly explained. | S7 | 5 |
